Child Guidance Clinic

  • Our Child Guidance Clinic supports children and adolescents under the age of 18.
  • Children experiencing emotional or behavioural challenges can seek support at our clinic.
  • The clinic has trained psychiatrists specialising in child care who conduct workshops and sessions with paediatricians, special educators, and counsellors.
  • We provide comprehensive assessment, treatment, and intervention for children dealing with mood disorders, anxiety, and other emotional conditions that require special attention

Centre for Children with Special Needs

  • This centre provides holistic care for children with developmental disabilities and disorders.
  • Our team includes paediatricians, orthopaedic specialists, general physicians, rehabilitation specialists, child psychologists, psychiatrists, ENT specialists, ophthalmologists, dentists, physiotherapists, speech therapists, special educators, and medico-social workers.
  • A thorough assessment is conducted before recommending any treatment.
  • Parents are guided and counselled separately to support their child’s needs.

Community Outreach Services

  • Our community outreach services aim to provide accessible and high-quality healthcare.
  • Our mission is to bridge healthcare gaps, promote wellness, and empower individuals to take charge of their health.
  • Services include primary care, preventive healthcare, and daycare facilities.
  • Basic laboratory services are also available

Body Donation

  • Adichunchanagiri Hospital runs a voluntary body donation scheme in collaboration with the Department of Anatomy at Adichunchanagiri Institute of Medical Sciences.
  • Under this scheme, individuals can voluntarily pledge to donate their bodies after death.
  • Donated bodies are used for medical education and research purposes.
  • This initiative helps medical students gain a better understanding of the human body and various pathological conditions.
  • The hospital also offers organ and tissue donation services.

Mortuary Services

  • Adichunchanagiri Hospital provides mortuary services for the safe preservation of deceased individuals until they are taken for cremation
  • Bodies are stored in cold room conditions and can be preserved for up to 48 hours.
  • The hospital also offers embalming services for long-term body preservation

Body Embalming Services

  • Embalming is the process of disinfecting, sanitising, and preserving a deceased body.
  • A formaldehyde-based chemical solution is used to slow down decomposition.
  • Embalming allows for the postponement of cremation in case of delays in funeral arrangements.
  • In many cases, embalming is legally required for bodies being transported by air.

Disaster Relief Services

  • At Adichunchanagiri Hospital, we understand that disasters can strike at any time. We have a dedicated disaster relief unit ready to respond immediately when needed.
  • Our disaster relief unit welcomes volunteers to assist in relief efforts.
  • Volunteers are trained in key protocols, including primary healthcare, food and water hygiene, water disinfection, environmental hygiene (temporary shelters and waste disposal), and psychosocial support.
  • Our goal is to minimise mortality and morbidity and ensure that affected individuals feel safe and protected.

Haematology & Stem Cell Transplant Centre

  • Our Haematology Centre provides specialised care for blood cancers and blood-related disorders.
  • We offer treatments for various conditions, including anaemia, bone marrow aplasia, coagulation disorders, myeloma, lymphoma, and acute and chronic leukaemia.
  • The centre supports and coordinates all transplant-related activities.
  • We also have a daycare facility where cancer patients can receive outpatient chemotherapy.
  • Blood transfusion services and biological and cellular therapy are available.

Lactation Clinic

  • The Lactation Clinic provides counselling and support for breastfeeding mothers.
  • We assist in lactation management for antenatal and postnatal patients
  • Breastfeeding sessions are observed and assessed to provide better guidance to nursing mothers
  • We provide specialised care for underweight and sick newborns
  • Counselling is available for mothers struggling with low milk supply or breastfeeding difficulties.

Natural Family Planning Clinic

  • The clinic offers one-on-one counselling sessions to help couples plan pregnancies naturally
  • We also conduct training programmes on fertility awareness in rural and urban communities.
  • Value education and fertility awareness sessions are organised for school and college students

Nutrition Clinic

  • The Nutrition Clinic at Adichunchanagiri Hospital provides dietary counselling for both adult and paediatric inpatients and outpatients.
  • We offer guidance on dietary management for conditions such as obesity, diabetes, cardiovascular disorders, liver diseases, renal disorders, and metabolic conditions.
  • Specialised tube feeding and parenteral nutrition are provided for hospital inpatients with specific dietary needs.
  • A complete nutritional assessment is conducted, followed by personalised dietary and lifestyle advice.

Dietary Services

  • Our Dietary Services unit is led by expert dietitians with extensive experience.
  • They provide comprehensive diet plans for individuals seeking better health or managing medical conditions.
  • Regular awareness sessions are held to educate patients on the importance of a balanced diet in disease prevention.
  • Specialised therapeutic diet plans are also provided based on medical requirements.

Senior Citizen Program

  • The Senior Citizen Programme aims to promote, preserve, and maintain the health of older adults in rural and urban areas of Mandya.
  • Our community service initiatives are led by geriatric medicine experts.
  • Services include home visits and medical check-ups at subsidised rates
  • Awareness campaigns are conducted in collaboration with AIMS to highlight geriatric health issues.

Ambulance

  • Adichunchanagiri Hospital provides 24/7 ambulance services.
  • Our ambulances are well-equipped to handle medical emergencies.
  • Patients can avail themselves of this service by calling our emergency helpline number.

Orthotics & Prosthetics

The Orthotics & Prosthetics Facility at Adichunchanagiri Hospital provides custom-made devices such as:

  • Lower limb orthoses
  • Spinal orthoses
  • Upper limb orthoses
  • Head supports (e.g., cervical collars, four-post collars)

We also offer a wide range of prostheses, including:

  • Symes prosthesis
  • Trans-tibial prosthesis
  • Trans-femoral prosthesis
  • Below-elbow prosthesis
  • Above-elbow prosthesis
  • Cosmetic prostheses
