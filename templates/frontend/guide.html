Registration Details

At Adichunchanagiri Hospital, registration is easy and accessible.

  • We have one main registration counter at the hospital's entrance and 10 other counters across the campus.
  • These counters are open throughout the day to serve you without delays.
  • A nominal fee of ₹30 is charged for registration.
  • You’ll receive an OPD card after registration, which is valid for one year.
  • You can renew your card easily using the same unique ID provided during your first visit.
  • Please carry your OPD card and any medical reports every time you visit the hospital.

For any help or information, feel free to approach the ‘May I Help You’ desk at the main entrance.

Admission Guidelines

If a doctor advises you to get admitted:

  • You’ll be guided to the Admission Counter for further steps, including counselling about your hospital stay and completing formalities.
  • The hospital has 1151 beds for inpatients, including 120 beds for critical care
  • We offer a range of ward options to suit your needs: General Ward, Semi-Special Ward, Private Single Rooms (A/C & None A/C), Deluxe Rooms, Suite Rooms

Our team will assist you in choosing the best option based on your preferences and availability.
