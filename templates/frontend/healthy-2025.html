Debunking the Myths with No-Nonsense Facts!

There’s a surge of health myths going around, often a combination of outdated ideas and impossible health goals preached by self-proclaimed health gurus. Today let’s tackle these head-on, one myth at a time.

8 Glasses of Water a Day to Keep Health Issues at Bay!

The National Institute of Medicine has set an Adequate Intake (AI) level for total water, this level is assumed to meet the daily water requirements of an individual. However, there are a number of factors within and around you that can influence this value. So, even though 8 glasses are ideal, maybe it isn’t ideal for your body.

Frozen vegetables have no nutrition!

Frozen veggies are as nutritious as their fresh counterparts! And sometimes a little more than the fresh ones, if they are frozen right after the harvest.

10,000 steps a day: Benchmark for good health!

There is no scientifically proven ‘magic number’ to be fit and healthy. However, the underlying truth is, everyone needs some kind of moderate to rigorous exercise to stay fit and healthy. This depends on age, lifestyle and overall health of an individual.

Organic Products = Natural Products!

Although organic and natural are used synonymously by the masses, they are actually two different things. When a product has the label ‘natural’ on it, it indicates the product being devoid of artificial colours, preservatives etc; whereas organic means the food has been produced without synthetic pesticides or fertilizers.

Choose complex carbs over the refined ones

Refined carbs are highly processed, stripping away their fiber and essential nutrients, making them less beneficial for your body. When consumed in excess, these carbs can contribute to overeating, weight gain, and other health issues. Opting for complex, unprocessed carbs instead can provide more nutrients and support better health.
