Patient Rights & Responsibilities

  1. ADICHUNCHANAGIRI HOSPITAL & RESEARCH CENTRE

Rights of Patient

At Adichunchanagiri Hospital, we believe that every patient deserves to be treated with dignity, fairness, and respect. We are committed to ensuring a safe and ethical healthcare environment where no patient faces discrimination or mistreatment.

Patients at our hospital have the right to:

  • Access quality healthcare without discrimination.
  • Maintain privacy during medical examinations, procedures, or treatments.
  • Be protected from physical abuse, neglect, or mistreatment.
  • Expect confidentiality of their medical information and personal details.
  • Refuse treatment after understanding the risks and consequences.
  • Raise concerns or file complaints regarding any injustice or malpractice.
  • Participate in treatment decisions and be fully informed about their medical care.
  • Receive transparent information about treatment costs and hospital facilities.
  • Access their past medical records maintained by the hospital.

Responsibilities of Patient

At Adichunchanagiri Hospital, we believe that responsible participation in healthcare ensures a smooth and effective treatment process. We encourage all patients to adhere to the following responsibilities to maintain a safe and supportive environment:

  • Communicate Concerns- Inform hospital staff about any safety issues or concerns regarding your treatment.
  • Provide Informed Consent- Give consent for admission, treatment, and medical procedures after understanding the details.
  • Avoid Bringing Valuables- Refrain from bringing expensive items to the hospital to prevent loss or theft.
  • Follow Hospital Rules- Avoid smoking, consuming alcohol, or engaging in activities that violate hospital policies.
  • Respect Others- Be considerate of fellow patients, visitors, and hospital staff by maintaining a peaceful environment.
  • Make Reasonable Requests- Refrain from making demands that may disrupt hospital operations or patient care.
  • Share Accurate Medical Information- Provide complete details about your medical history and ask questions if you need clarification.
  • Adhere to Treatment Plans- Follow prescribed treatments and cooperate with healthcare professionals for better recovery.
  • Maintain Cleanliness- Help keep hospital premises clean and hygienic for the safety of all.
  • Settle Payments Promptly- Ensure timely payment of hospital charges for continued and uninterrupted care.
