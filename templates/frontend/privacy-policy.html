Adichunchanagiri Hospital- Privacy Policy

Adichunchanagiri Hospital is committed to maintaining the privacy of your medical information and ensuring that all personal data is kept confidential. This Privacy Policy applies to the information collected or displayed on our website. We take privacy seriously and assure you that the information you provide to us will remain confidential unless otherwise required by law. We will not intentionally share any personal information with third parties unless required by law. Please note that we do not guarantee or assure that electronic communications you send to us or records accessed may not be accessed by third parties.

Communication

Adichunchanagiri Hospital may contact you via various communication channels such as phone, SMS, online messaging platforms (including WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, etc.), and emails. This communication may include appointment reminders, medical history updates, doctor requests, and general updates about Adichunchanagiri Hospital and other properties operated by our institution.

If you do not wish to receive any communications, you can either communicate this directly to the caller or unsubscribe via the link at the bottom of any email. Your request will be processed within 2 weeks.

Data Collected

We collect anonymous data from visitors to our website or application to monitor traffic and resolve server issues. For example, we may collect information such as web requests, the data returned in response, IP addresses, browser types, browser languages, and time stamps for requests.

Cookies may be used to store session information for your convenience across browsers, mobile devices, and other platforms. We also use third-party tools to enhance your browsing experience, and their privacy policies are independent of our website or application.

Use of Data

We only use your personal information to provide services and communicate with you about services or updates related to Adichunchanagiri Hospital. We take the privacy and confidentiality of any information you upload to our platform seriously and use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access.

Adichunchanagiri Hospital may contact you via email or other means regarding your use of the hospital’s services or website. For example, we may send promotional emails about our services or third-party offerings we believe may interest you. We may also use technology to confirm when you open an email from us. You can modify your email preferences at any time by clicking the appropriate link at the bottom of any email from us. If you no longer wish to receive emails from Adichunchanagiri Hospital, please opt-out using the unsubscribe link in the email footer.

Changes in the Privacy Policy

Adichunchanagiri Hospital reserves the right to amend this privacy and security policy at any time. Any updates will be posted on our website and at our hospital facilities. By continuing to use our website, you agree to the updated Privacy Policy and its provisions. We recommend regularly reviewing this policy to stay informed about any changes.

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the information we collect on our website. We do not extend this policy to third-party websites, even if they are linked to or from our website. We make no guarantees regarding the privacy or security of any personal information you provide to third-party websites.
